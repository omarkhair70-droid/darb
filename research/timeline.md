# Darb 1718 — Evidence-led timeline (2001–2026)

Research freeze: 2026-11-02. **Verified vs disputed statements are marked.** Dates refer to events, not the publication date of this file.

## 2001–2008: an idea becomes a place

- **2001 (reported origin):** Founder Moataz Nasr says he conceived a cross-disciplinary arts and community space after experiences with international cultural spaces. [Moleskine Foundation](https://moleskinefoundation.org/creativity-pioneer/the-fight-to-save-darb-1718/).
- **2002 (reported proposal):** Reporting in Al Jazeera says a project was presented to Cairo's governor for land near Fustat's pottery community. [Al Jazeera, 2024](https://www.aljazeera.net/culture/2024/1/14/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%85-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D8%B2%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2).
- **2007–2008 (important discrepancy):** Moleskine records an opening in 2007, an early government closure and reopening in 2008. The institution's 2026 overview and multiple artist bios say **founded 2008**; Anna Lindh's directory says 2010 (likely registration or directory convention, *not confirmed*). Use **2008 established** in public summaries while acknowledging these differences. [Moleskine](https://moleskinefoundation.org/creativity-pioneer/the-fight-to-save-darb-1718/), [Sharjah Art Foundation](https://www.sharjahart.org/en/people/details/nasr-moataz), [Anna Lindh](https://alf.website/en/?members=darb-1718-contemporary-art-and-culture-center).
- The name **17–18** refers, according to a contemporary interview with Nasr, to the **January 17–18, 1977 protests**. [Ahram Online, March 2011](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/32/7917/Folk/Folk-arts-Rocks-and-ballots-at-Darb-.aspx).

## 2010–2013: memory and civic dialogue

- **Dec 2010–Jan 2011:** Nabil Boutros's *Egyptians* was exhibited at Darb as part of *Fames Family Vaudeville*. After the Alexandria church bombing, Nasr made an **All Egyptians** solidarity poster with the photographs, supported by 20 cultural institutions. [Nafas/Universes in Universe](https://universes.art/en/nafas/articles/2011/nabil-boutros).
- **March 2011:** Darb staged an exhibition engaging with the Jan 25 revolution. Nasr archived placards from Tahrir, later displayed locally and in Amsterdam. [Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/32/7917/Folk/Folk-arts-Rocks-and-ballots-at-Darb-.aspx), [Mediamatic](https://www.mediamatic.net/en/page/21094/25th-january).
- **March 2013:** *Recording Against Regimes* connected film and art about uprisings in Egypt, Germany and Poland. [Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/UI/Front/Inner.aspx?NewsContentID=66922).

## 2015–2022: independent cultural infrastructure

- **2015 and 2018:** editions of **Something Else**, an independent contemporary-art initiative developed as a response to disrupted official biennials. Institution's 2026 overview PDF, pp. 15–18; [2018 participants, Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/5/312718/Arts--Culture/Darb-s-Something-Else--Off-Biennale-A-full-list-of.aspx).
- **2020–2022:** activities were disrupted during the pandemic; the **3031 Festival**, March 2022, marked a return after approximately two years without normal activities, involving 37 visual artists and 25 performers, per contemporary coverage. [Al Ahram Hebdo](https://french.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/54/2352/36416/AlAhram-Hebdo/Arts/Les-arts-au-rendezvous.aspx).

## 2023–2024: threat and demolition

- **July–August 2023:** Darb and neighbouring pottery workshops faced urgent evacuation orders in the context of road/bridge development. Nasr filed a legal challenge and publicly advocated alternatives. The government/district position was not fully available in the reporting reviewed; attribute disputed details to their source. [Mada Masr Aug 3](https://www.madamasr.com/en/2023/08/03/feature/politics/in-fustat-cultural-center-darb-1718-pottery-factories-face-demolition/), [The Art Newspaper Sep 14](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/09/14/cairo-art-centre-faces-demolition-as-new-roads-threaten-heritage-sites).
- **Nov 23–Dec 23, 2023:** *Something Else* at the Citadel: **22 curators, 171 artists, 42 countries**, per organizer's 2026 overview PDF pp. 17–19. Artistic director Moataz Nasr, chief curator Simon Njami; theme *What Then?*
- **January 2–3, 2024:** reporting documents the demolition of Darb's principal building. Nasr alleged confiscation/damage to art and equipment; this is a reported claim rather than independently inventoried loss. [Mada Masr, Jan 3](https://www.madamasr.com/en/2024/01/03/news/u/main-building-demolished-at-darb-1718-cultural-center/).
- **After the demolition:** institution survives as an operation, not as its entire prior architectural footprint. An Anna Lindh Foundation entry describes ~80% of physical space lost and a remaining exhibition/workshop programme; figures may reflect a particular period. [ALF profile](https://alf.website/en/?members=darb-1718-contemporary-art-and-culture-center).

## 2025–2026: continuation and rebuilding

- **Feb 8–22, 2025:** *Something Else Symposium* at Darb and Bayt Al Sinnari (overview PDF p. 16).
- **May 22–Jun 4, 2025:** *INBETWEEN: Thin Places, Movements, and Meetings* (p. 7).
- **June 2025:** Darb launched an **artist-supported art auction/fundraiser**, seeking funds toward a new, more sustainable headquarters. Auction supporters included George Bahgoury and Ahmed Morsi according to press. The article **does not establish a completed purchase/new headquarters**. [Bab Masr](https://babmsr.com/en/darb-1718/).
- **Aug 16–23, 2025:** *Observer Speaks: A Young Artists’ Guide to the World* (overview p. 8).
- **Nov 25–Dec 14, 2025:** *Darb fi Al-Hadiqqa / A Path in the Garden*, 17 artists, 2 workshops (p. 6).
- **2026:** *Something Else* activities at Bayt Al Sinnari; *Jusoor* short film programme, workshops, residencies, and *UNSPOKEN* September 12–25 (overview pp. 5, 9–15).
- **Sep 2026:** public internship call for applications through Sep 30, further indicating current operation. [Darb social repost](https://www.findglocal.com/EG/Cairo/146310505389760/Darb-1718).
- **Nov 2026:** the official domain `darb1718.com` was found returning a hosting-account suspension page during this research check. Social links in [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/darb1718) remain important; **do not infer the organization closed** from a suspended website.
