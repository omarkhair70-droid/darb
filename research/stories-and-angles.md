# Human stories and editorial arcs — NOT fabricated biographies

This document keeps **evidence** separate from **creative framing**. A dramatic story can be constructed from chronology and testimony; no private motivations or invented quotes.

## 1. The place that was an idea before it was a building
**Verified backbone:** Moataz Nasr developed the idea around 2001, drew support to open by 2007–08, and connected contemporary art with Fustat's potters and artisans. The institution's initial opening was reportedly interrupted by an early closure. [Moleskine Foundation](https://moleskinefoundation.org/creativity-pioneer/the-fight-to-save-darb-1718/). [Ahram 2023 profile](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/50/506648/AlAhram-Weekly/A-fairy-tale-for-our-times.aspx).
**Potential narrative:** A space founded to connect worlds; the city itself later tests whether this kind of space can survive. **Don't write** that authorities intentionally opposed the mission unless independently evidenced.

## 2. Seventeen, eighteen: the name and the politics of public memory
**Verified backbone:** In a 2011 interview Nasr explained the name as a reference to the January 17–18, 1977 protests. In March 2011 the institution exhibited imagery and material related to the Egyptian revolution. [Ahram 2011](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/32/7917/Folk/Folk-arts-Rocks-and-ballots-at-Darb-.aspx).
**Visual approach:** typographic 17 / 18, streets, original posters, archive fragments—rights and context cleared first.

## 3. Art as witness, not decoration
**Verified backbone:** Artist Nabil Boutros's portraits formed part of the 2010–11 *Egyptians* exhibit. After the Alexandria church bombing, Nasr adapted them into a solidarity poster distributed via cultural institutions. [Nafas](https://universes.art/en/nafas/articles/2011/nabil-boutros).
**Narrative:** An image leaves the gallery and becomes a statement in the street. Avoid identifying photographed participants beyond published captions.

## 4. The pandemic return that preceded another loss
**Verified backbone:** *3031 Festival* in 2022 marked renewed activity after a roughly two-year pandemic hiatus, involving 37 visual artists and 25 performers, as reported at the time. [Al Ahram Hebdo](https://french.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/54/2352/36416/AlAhram-Hebdo/Arts/Les-arts-au-rendezvous.aspx).
**Narrative:** The center learned to reopen once—then had to face an entirely different rupture.

## 5. Thirty minutes, a threatened demolition, then the loss
**Verified backbone:** According to Nasr's September 2023 account, officials gave a very short evacuation deadline. Coverage documents a legal challenge and the January 2024 demolition of the principal building. [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/09/14/cairo-art-centre-faces-demolition-as-new-roads-threaten-heritage-sites), [Mada Masr](https://www.madamasr.com/en/2024/01/03/news/u/main-building-demolished-at-darb-1718-cultural-center/).
**Narrative:** Concrete disappears; an institution and relationships survive. **Caution:** the reported treatment of art and devices represents Nasr's account; do not present unaudited quantities or legal allegations as judicial findings.

## 6. Artists give back
**Verified backbone:** In June 2025 artists contributed works for a fundraiser/auction aiming to finance a new home. The founder publicly emphasized the response; press named George Bahgoury and Ahmed Morsi. [Bab Masr](https://babmsr.com/en/darb-1718/).
**Narrative:** People once given space to make art gave art back so the space might live. **Unknown:** amount raised and whether a property was acquired.

## 7. From crisis to new partners, without a false happy ending
**Verified backbone:** February 10 2025, a partnership MoU with **PARAGON Developments** was announced during *Something Else Symposium*, with 44 artists and 10 curators from 15 countries. [Zawya press release](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/paragon-and-darb-1718-an-inspiring-partnership-to-empower-the-arts-with-a-sustainable-theme-ezccdpc0). Darb's 2026 programme demonstrates continued activity, but **we did not verify a completed new standalone headquarters**.
**Narrative:** Survival is sustained by a network of artists, institutions, funders, and neighbours—not a miraculous one-day comeback.

## 8. Make the neighbours central
**Verified backbone:** *Tales of Fustat* is deliberately structured around artisans, photo-walks, portraits, cyanotype and handwritten stories (2026 overview p.11). The historic Darb identity was built around Fustat's craft neighbourhood.
**Narrative:** Clay, hand, voice, print, story. **Consent:** participants' individual names/stories need releases or direct publication.

## 9. Art and recovery: careful language
**Verified backbone:** *UNSPOKEN* (September 2026) featured works created through art-therapy sessions with participants in recovery; institution PDF p.5 and the [public event notice](https://www.evepla.com/EG/Cairo/146310505389760/Darb-1718).
**Narrative:** A space for expressing what is difficult to say. Do not sensationalize mental health, imply cures, or reveal identities.

## 10. Nourhan's documented contribution (NOT a personal backstory)
The overview PDF metadata lists **Nourhan Elmeligy** as document author; curator Power Ekroth publicly credits the same full name among contributors to a 2026 Something Else project: [No Problem: The People](https://www.powerekroth.net/noproblemthepeople). This supports a *possible institutional/creative contributor story*, **not a full personal biography**. Interview and role verification needed before writing a profile.

## Suggested documentary structure
**Opening:** black-and-white old Fustat + sound of potters → **Act 1:** community builds a cultural space → **Act 2:** protests, experiments and film/art network → **Act 3:** the threat, physical demolition, uncertainty → **Act 4:** relationships and art-led fundraising → **Present:** surviving programmes, questions still open.

**Do not use the phrase “they rebuilt the building” without a confirmed site/date.** More accurate: **they kept the institution and artistic mission active**.
