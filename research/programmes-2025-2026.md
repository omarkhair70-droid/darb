## Ongoing / undated strands in the PDF
- **Jusoor / جسور:** platform for short films and filmmaker/audience discussions; special Sudanese screening of *Dukhan* (Mohamed Subahi), *Braids of Identity* (Hafsa Baraei), *The Last Henna* (Mohannad Bilal), *Motive* (Alargam Muzmail). Also an Iranian short-film collaboration with Left Bank Cinema Collective. (p.9)
- **Natalia Ossef residency:** researched Egypt's textile and craft practices through Ramses Wissa Wassef Art Center, tentmaking/khayameya around Bab Zuweila, and Tunis Village in Fayoum; concluded with an open studio. (p.10)
- **Tales of Fustat / حكاوي الفسطاط:** 2-session cyanotype programme; neighbourhood photo walk, craftspeople interviews and portraits, hand-printed photographic notebooks with written stories. (p.11)
- **A Room of One's Own / غرفة تخصنا وحدنا:** 4-week writing programme guided by Aisha Aljaedy, discussing home, private space, gender and boundaries. (p.12)
- **Voice in Motion / الحركة في الصوت:** voice, listening, improvisation, movement, connection. (p.13)
- **Alexandria Biennale:** referenced in overview as returning after **12 years**, curated by Moataz Nasr under *This Too Shall Pass* (p.14). This is an **associated-sector event**, not a claim Darb alone organized it.
- **Something Else:** overview identifies editions in **2015, 2018, 2023, 2025 symposium, 2026** (pp.15–18). At the Citadel Nov 23–Dec 23 2023: 22 curators, 171 artists, 42 countries, theme *What Then?* (pp.17–18).
- **Planning sketches:** outdoor, first, second floors by Rokaya Abdelhafez (p.21). **Proposals/plan images**, not automatically completed floor plans.

## Additional independently reported 2026 details
*UNSPOKEN* was conceived and curated by **Salma Hegazi** in collaboration with art therapist **Shady Ghaly**, according to a Darb event notice mirrored [here](https://www.evepla.com/EG/Cairo/146310505389760/Darb-1718).

## Responsible storytelling note
Art therapy, mental health and recovery participants must not be identified from images or attributed private stories without consent. Use the programme's institution-level framing and public statements only.
