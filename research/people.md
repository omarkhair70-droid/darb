## Moataz Nasr / معتز نصر
- Founder and artistic director of Darb 1718; internationally exhibiting, self-taught multidisciplinary Egyptian artist (sculpture, photo, video, painting), background in economics. [Sharjah Art Foundation](https://www.sharjahart.org/en/people/details/nasr-moataz).
- Creator of the initial idea (reported 2001), commentator on 2023–24 demolition and 2025 fundraiser. [Moleskine](https://moleskinefoundation.org/creativity-pioneer/the-fight-to-save-darb-1718/); [Bab Masr](https://babmsr.com/en/darb-1718/).
- Artist institutional profiles: [Galleria Continua](https://www.galleriacontinua.com/artists/moataz-nasr-51), [Sharjah](https://www.sharjahart.org/en/people/details/nasr-moataz).
