# Darb 1718 — Institution, place, artistic DNA

## Identity
Independent Egyptian contemporary art / cultural centre and registered non-profit, founded by artist **Moataz Nasr** in Fustat/Old Cairo. Founding year used by institution: **2008** (noting contradictory opening/registration dates).

**Why Fustat matters:** Contemporary exhibitions coexisted with local pottery, sculpture, ceramics, glass and metal crafts. The neighbourhood is also close to Coptic, Roman and Islamic historical monuments. A spatial and social relationship with artisans was part of the mission, not merely scenic background.

## Mission extracted from supplied 2026 Overview (p. 2)
1. Spaces/resources for exhibitions, concerts, festivals, independent film screenings, workshops.
2. Participatory arts education, particularly underrepresented groups.
3. Integrate local craft practitioners into contemporary arts.
4. Community engagement through art.
5. Guidance/training for creativity and expression.
6. Experimental/underground music and innovative art forms.
7. International artist residencies and co-creation.

## Programme strands (overview pp. 3–13)
- Exhibitions / residencies; concerts / screenings; community outreach; workshops / crafts.
- Film discussions with filmmakers and audience via **Jusoor**.
- Handmaking, oral histories and printing; interdisciplinary, participatory encounters.
- Cross-cultural dialogue including Sudanese and Iranian film nights.

## Historic space (pre-2024; must not advertise as current)
Former facility included two gallery spaces, performance stages, outdoor cinema, gardens, rooftop, and residency facilities. [Arts Collaboratory](https://artscollaboratory.org/darb-1718/); [TransArtists](https://www.transartists.org/en/air/darb-1718) (historic specifications, *not current*).
The supplied PDF p.21 shows **architectural 2D sketches** labelled outdoor space, first floor, second floor, credited to **Rokaya Abdelhafez**. Neither scale nor completion/current implementation should be assumed.

## Institution structure (source date unknown)
An [Anna Lindh Foundation profile](https://alf.website/en/?members=darb-1718-contemporary-art-and-culture-center) lists artistic director/founder **Moataz Nasr**, managing director **Farah Nasr**, and programmes/operations manager **Shorouk El Hariry**, with reported 5–10 staff and circa USD 50k annual budget. These appear to be self-reported or directory data and may have changed; do not present as 2026 audited accounts.

## Strong original distinctions
- **A bridge between artisans and contemporary artists**, not a white-cube gallery alone.
- **Cultural gathering as infrastructure**: stage, courtyard, learning, films, sound, crafts.
- **Historic city / new artistic practice** in deliberate tension.
- **Continuity without uninterrupted occupancy**: survive 2007/2008, pandemic and demolition.
- **Something Else** as self-organised international art dialogue and alternative to cancelled official biennials.
