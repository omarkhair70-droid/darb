# Brand Archaeology — Darb 1718 printed / programme identity sources

**Research date:** 2 Nov 2026. A source register of **existing** (not generated) visual identities and posters, to distinguish institution marks from programme voices and speculative student/independent exercises.

## Evidence key
- `A` directly supplied Darb Overview 2026.
- `B` published programme/promotional visual attributed to Darb or participating institutions; not proof of permanent brand standards.
- `C` third-party documentation or arts journalism.
- `U` an unverified branding experiment; do NOT publish as approved official identity.

## Visual material evidence

### 01 — Institutional mark in 2026 Overview
**A**, PDF p.1 and p.3/p.4/p.20. Circular Darb symbol / name represented in multiple PDF embedded objects (#2, #8, #9, #72). One consistent mark is present but no complete logo usage guide, negative space spec or clear vector master is supplied.

**Design implication:** Establish an institutional **recognition anchor** and request official vector master / original files; test whether to retain it unchanged. Do not draw a new logo and call it rebrand.

### 02 — Something Else / Structural Muteness poster (2026)
**B/C**, poster hosted with [Artsy viewing room](https://www.artsy.net/viewing-room/aaie-gallery-something-else-2026-structural-muteness). Red/white textured architectural motifs and bold Arabic-English typographic collision; logos of participating institutions. Programme named Something Else 2026, with Bayt Al-Sinnari and Darb venues. It is a **programme-specific visual identity**, not necessarily the wider Darb corporate website palette.

**Design implication:** Study typographic tension between scripts and ornamental architectural quotation, but do **not transplant red+white arches** onto every Darb website page. Ensure artwork remains visually sovereign and partner marks don't swamp core navigation.

### 03 — Aouda / عودة visual campaign (2022)
**C**, [SceneNow event listing](https://scenenow.com/Events/Detail/15804-Aouda-Contemporary-Exhibition), which carries a poster with saturated red, blue type, wing/flame imagery and an English “Rising from the Ashes” line.
- The event was listed for **Nov 3–Dec 3 2022**, describing a “grand re-opening” and an anniversary.
- **Crucial:** This material pre-dates the **Jan 2024** demolition. It cannot be used as evidence of a 2024 response or a verified new building.
- **Artistic clue:** Darb's vocabulary already contained transformation, re-opening and expressive graphic typography before 2024.

**Design implication:** Resist an easy “destroyed then reborn” script. Research what **Aouda 2022** actually meant before asking team to narrate an uninterrupted arc.

### 04 — 2020 Darb branding exploration by Nadine Farag
**U**, [Behance project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/101771853/Darb-1718), published Aug 2 2020. Typographic/brand study by named designer **Nadine Farag**.
- No verified evidence this was approved for permanent implementation or connected to current Darb leadership.
- Study as **design research**, not official identity. Ask the team if they know it.

### 05 — 2024 (A)Part from the City exhibition poster
**C**, [Cairo360 programme listing](https://www.cairo360.com/event-new/apart-from-the-city-photography-exhibition/). Black-and-white map/typographic grid, event July 18–31 2024. The event poster uses an entirely different identity from Aouda and 2026 Something Else.

**Design implication:** An exhibition's visual language must remain adaptable; a cultural website should **host programme identities**, not flatten them into single corporate colors.

### 06 — Existing photographic archive vs polished campaign
**A**, 2026 Overview image set, especially pp.3/9/11/17/20: documentary workshop/cinema/performance photographs, artwork reproduction, formal exhibition spaces and ambient audiences. Image quality ranges dramatically; 77 extracted objects, not 77 coherent campaign images.

**Design implication:** A modular photo presentation needs rights, credit and consistent display states, not identical masks/crops. A project editor should preserve original poster art as image with readable alt/caption alongside accessible HTML event information.

### 07 — Longstanding web/archival ambition
**B/C**, [Khatt Foundation directory](https://www.khtt.net/en/page/14055/darb-1718) described Darb's role as developing and maintaining a database/archive of Egyptian and MENA artists, plus workshops and community programmes.
**Interpretation:** An active digital archive is not an invented ‘award-site’ layer; it echoes an ambition documented in Darb's own earlier institutional descriptions. The scope and completeness of such a database today remain unverified.

### 08 — Real operational offerings visible Nov 2026
**B**, public [Darb Linktree](https://linktr.ee/darb1718) includes active-looking calls/forms for weekly pottery, UNSPOKEN, short film submissions, internships, artist residencies, and rental hall, alongside social accounts.
**Interpretation:** New site should preserve **service journeys** even if storytelling is highly authored. Links may be old; verify deadline and live status before using as current CTA.

## Major finding
**One institution, many artistic voices.** The permanent identity should play the role of *reliable host/publisher* (navigation, credits, language, date, event status); exhibitions are *featured authors* (their own poster/type/colour where rights permit). The identity system is more like an editorial framework or house style than one hard-coded art direction for every artwork.

## Publication rules
- All linked external posters/images are **source URL references**; they are not automatically permission-cleared for rehosting or manipulating in this GitHub repo.
- Do not conflate independent Darb-named businesses/branding projects with Darb 1718.
- Authentic official SVG logo and 2026 working event posters should be requested from Darb.
- For poster-led programme pages, render essential text (date, venue, registration, access) as **accessible structured fields**, not text burned into a raster image.
- For historical programmes, expose their original dates and archive status, never suggest old event links still accept bookings.
