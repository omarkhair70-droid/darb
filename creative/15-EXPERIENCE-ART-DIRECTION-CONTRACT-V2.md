# Darb 1718 — Whole-site experience and artistic direction contract V2

Working hypothesis, NOT a selected look, final slogan, approved information architecture or authorized artwork reproduction. Created in response to full client rejection of Vercel V1, 2 Nov 2026.

## 1 / Cultural premise to test
The Darb source describes a living artistic ecosystem connecting Fustat's craftspeople, Egyptian and international contemporary art, film, artist residencies, exhibitions, conversations and making. It is NOT primarily an archive, a museum of historical objects, a digital brochure, a memorial to the demolished building, nor a portfolio with a new logo.

Proposition for debate: the website should enable **encountering, looking, tracing, gathering and participating**. These are verbs / user experiences, not names of five pages or prescribed colors.

## 2 / What the visitor can actually DO, not how the homepage looks
- ENCOUNTER: start with one **real** credited work, artist, process, screening, exhibition or current program in its own medium. No obligatory large slogan, rectangle, or artistically irrelevant first-screen banner. Investigate whether we even need a conventional homepage.
- LOOK: inspect authentic works with context, scale, media-specific presentation and full artist and photographer credits. Curator-authored questions may help sustained viewing, but not a chatbot invented to seem sophisticated.
- TRACE: move between verified artwork, credited maker, project, place, event and related practices; each link explicitly marked as documented historical relation or editorial comparison. Return/back works; keep URLs shareable and modes linked.
- GATHER: current screenings, workshops, artist calls, openings and participation should be immediately accessible through approved actual dates, venue and links; no fake current activities.
- CONTRIBUTE: consider artist-/curator-authored annotations, sound and responses only after consent and editorial review. Not user-generated fiction about artists.
- READ QUIETLY: captions, searchable index, keyboard, AR/EN, reduced-motion and image alternatives always work. No 3D world as the exclusive mode.

## 3 / One cultural content system
Each published entity must carry: type, source evidence, AR/EN content, media and its rights, date and actual historic/current place, maker/credit and publication status.

Core entities:
- Artworks: project, author, title, medium, original image/video/audio, capture photographer, usage permissions, full caption, translation, high-res/source.
- Projects/exhibitions: curatorial proposition, original show identity where available, verified artists and works, exact location and dates, images, event history.
- People: only documented public contributions; artist, maker, curator, director, photographer and facilitator roles are NOT interchangeable.
- Events and opportunities: status, timezone, confirmed venue, start and close dates, working form and contact.
- Places: historical vs currently open, provenance and last checked.
- Stories/scene beats: named works, why they belong together, cited connection, interaction purpose, captions and quiet/static equivalent.

**Important:** the current nine JSON project records do NOT amount to an artwork library or complete living program. Sanity has not been activated with real institutional staff or final media rights.

## 4 / A coherent visual language without one repeated layout
**Institutional anchor**: real Darb mark/identity approved by staff, readable wayfinding, AR/EN, credits, dates, event status, focus/search/access.

**Artist/program identity**: exhibitors and works can bring their own poster, typography, audiovisual rhythm and atmosphere. Preserve poster identity instead of flattening each show to a custom color applied to the same card.

Media should control presentation:
- installation / spatial art: overview, place and scale, work detail, public caption; do not fabricate 3D architectural rooms.
- textile and pottery: process, material changes, tools, named makers and actual documentation; don't use clay or paper texture as universal background.
- film / performance: time, authorized moving image, transcript/subtitles, sound controls, screening context.
- poster / graphic work: integrity of its actual composition, original design credit and legible HTML text for schedules.
- research archive photo: photographer, venue, date, event and relation evidence.

No palette, typography, image grid, hero format, homepage structure or motion preset is approved at present. The earlier beige/off-white and huge type system is explicitly void. Arabic/English need independently authored rhythm and full mixed-script testing.

## 5 / International benchmark — transfer the problem-solving, not surface aesthetics
- National Gallery Imaginarium × Fabrique/Q42: curator questions enable SLOW LOOKING; its Three.js/R3F world, Micrio zoom, Sanity and selective AI animation serve that commissioned goal. Transfer artist/curator-authored attention, not floating paintings. https://www.q42.nl/en/work/imaginarium-national-gallery
- Getty × Resn, Tracing Art: 400 supplied images and intense content/UX choreography turned provenance research into three linked narrative chapters; parts use GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis and three.js. Transfer evidence-led relationships and controlled narrative orientation, not an artwork sphere. https://www.commarts.com/project/38649/tracing-art
- Oculi Mundi × Fabrique/Q42: challenged the existence of a normal homepage and menu; Explore and Research represent the same collection; CSS 3D can outperform canvas for meaningful DOM. Transfer native interaction logic and dual access, not a cosmos or awkward cursor. https://www.fabrique.com/cases/museums-and-heritage/oculi-mundi-the-eyes-of-the-world/ ; https://engineering.q42.nl/oculi-mundi/
- KW Berlin / Correspondence × Systemantics (Oct 2025): a systematic identity layer and intuitive layer that real artists can change with drawings/annotations; also redesign of web/archive. Transfer **artist authorship within the institution's digital language**, not scribble graphics as a new template. https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/info/scribbles
- Darb itself: Something Else 2026's printed bilingual red/architecture identity, Aouda 2022's expression and other exhibition posters have DIFFERENT visual voices. None alone determines Darb's permanent brand. https://www.artsy.net/viewing-room/aaie-gallery-something-else-2026-structural-muteness

## 6 / First three actual content journeys, not design themes
A: Fustat and the cyanotype craft process. Overview p.3 shows makers and place; p.11 shows Tales of Fustat images, cyanotypes and notebooks. These may be separate events/people; never claim they are a single documented process without verification. View a work, process, people with documented names, then a verified actual workshop if open.

B: Something Else 2023 to later cultural work. Overview p.16–19 documents 2023 Citadel works and later editions/activities; What Then? is a 2023 curatorial question, not Darb's permanent tagline. Artwork-first -> maker -> event -> venue and time -> independently related archive. Keep edition-number discrepancies visible.

C: Film and public discussion. Jusoor, PDF p.9, documents screenings and filmmaker discussions. Offer actual film still or authorised clip and credits when obtained; historic program detail and separately confirmed current call. No invented director names or live submission dates.

These cases must exist across entry, Explore, Archive, artist/project, Visit and What's On as **one joined experience**, not three segregated websites.

## 7 / Tooling is an implementation response to artistic purpose
Keep Next.js + TypeScript, Sanity-ready schema, verified media pipeline and QA. GSAP/ScrollTrigger can choreograph a REAL sequence; CSS for simple state; Lenis only if useful for rhythm and navigation; Three.js/R3F or CSS3D for genuinely spatial work; deep zoom only with hires permitted originals. All include mobile/keyboard and reduced-motion equivalent. No technology banned. None chosen because it makes something look award-winning.

## 8 / Creative and release gates
- Minimum three *specific* commissioned artwork/media packets: title, credited artist/photographer, actual content, rights, high-res original, curatorial context and dates.
- Staff-verified current exhibition/workshop/opportunity and up-to-date venue for participation utility; otherwise visibly unknown.
- A connected full-site storyboard showing how one art encounter leads to artist/context, activity, archive and back; Arabic and English variants.
- Design interaction grammar by medium, not the site's eventual static stylesheet. Artistic review should critique *meaning* as well as original composition.
- No invented programme, image/artist treatment, fake source identity, obligatory cinematic intro or pre-selected layout.
- Mobile/desktop source-first QA, accessibility and performance; editorial CMS review workflow must genuinely work.
- Client signs off on a coherent artistic approach before claiming "new Darb website"; initial V1 is NOT that.

All currently approved facts remain in source-material and research. This document is a research contract, not a command to produce one predetermined visual solution.
