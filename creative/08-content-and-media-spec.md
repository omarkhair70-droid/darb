## Editorial production pipeline
1. Ingest image preserving exact source, checksum, xref and page.
2. Define event/project ID; photo ID; artist; photographer (if approved); location; date and usage scope.
3. Tag visual role: cover, detail, illustration, historical, portrait, stage, diagram.
4. Write curatorial caption and **alt in Arabic and English**, no implied identity/medical state.
5. Approve image crop; preserve full unmodified artwork image on detail page.
6. Generate serving variants by size, format, crop and focal point; raw originals retained.
7. Connect relational archive entities and source citations.
8. Editor preview, rights review, bilingual review, scheduled publish.
