## 2. One full source-grounded editorial walk: JUSOOR / جسور

**Not a homepage or storyboard of boxes.** The walk establishes what the visitor learns and can do, and what the art author must supply to make it truly experiential.

### Visitor's entry conditions
They might arrive from a shared film page, a Darb programme link, or the institution's current programme list. They never have to pass through a cinematic tunnel. AR/EN content and quick Index access are available throughout.

**Moment 0 — real identity**
- Public source claim: Jusoor is a film programme platform for short films and conversations, documented on p.9.
- Visitor sees: programme title (Arabic/English as in source), historical/unverified date status, medium “Film / Screening & discussion”, visible route to whole programme.
- Why: not a fabricated giant slogan or unrelated photograph of a crowd.
- Current media gate: the p.9 graphic is a small programme identity, **not** a licensed film still or a moving-image piece.

**Moment 1 — choose a named film, not an arbitrary photograph**
- Public source: film *Dukhan* with named maker **Mohamed Subahi**, in the p.9 Sudanese screening list.
- Visitor action: select this film within Jusoor. The active state is FILM → PROGRAMME with evidence citation.
- What must remain true: we **do not** say that Mohamed Subahi directed the other shorts, that every named director attended a discussion, or that this has a current screening date.
- Now: textual identity and programme record; later if approved: actual film still, filmmaker synopsis and trailer/excerpt, with the author choosing the media experience.

**Moment 2 — genuine attention through film language**
- We cannot invent questions about *Dukhan*'s scenes because the film and authorized interpretation are not in the supplied corpus.
- Editorial action to request: “Select an exact 15–30 second licensed excerpt (or one still), and write an artist-approved viewing question about a real cinematic detail.”
- If approved: real time-based playback with pause, native controls, captions/transcript, no autoplay and accurate credit. One detail could lead to a **maker-authored note**, but only if contributed.
- Without approval: display “Film material pending curator/filmmaker approval,” preserve programme title and full text. **No fake movie scene, still or animated generated interpretation.**

**Moment 3 — move through a documented relationship**
- Back to Jusoor programme from Dukhan. Other **separately documented** shorts appear: *Braids of Identity* (Hafsa Baraei), *The Last Henna* (Mohannad Bilal), *Motive* (Alargam Muzmail).
- User chooses one; each has its own director/film identity with p.9 evidence; the link is SAME PROGRAMME, not “same director,” “sequel” or “collaborated with”.
- An Iranian-short-films programme collaboration with Left Bank Cinema Collective can appear as another programme strand if correct source relation is documented. Do not flatten all cinema into one pan-Arab curator statement.

**Moment 4 — discover cultural context**
- User can ask “What is Jusoor?” and access source-based text on screenings and film/audience discussion. Exact panel and animation style are still open.
- If filmmaker commentary and individual screening date are missing, show the missing record state instead of a fabricated discussion, quote or date.
- Orientation allows returning to film or switching to full archival index with route state intact.

**Moment 5 — bridge into participation, honestly**
- The public Darb hub currently lists *Submit your short films* but its current acceptance/deadline needs direct verification. Show as an official-channel link, **not** “Apply now” unless staff confirms live status.
- A future confirmed event page must show exact venue/day/time and valid signup before showing a booking CTA. The former Darb Fustat building address is not automatically a live venue.
- Visitor exits with an **authentic** context path or current opportunity, not a generic contact CTA disguised as story climax.

### Evidence-to-interface action requirements
- Keep a context trail: e.g. FILM / Dukhan → PROGRAMME / Jusoor → DOCUMENT / Overview p.9.
- Every relation link is typed, publicly attributable and has an accessible equivalent.
- URL deep links must restore film and current context. Back/Forward returns to prior media state, not homepage.
- AR and EN share canonical IDs, but author copy is separately edited; audio and transcription language are recorded explicitly.
- Low-motion and text-only modes preserve the entire content and research links.
- Only film authors/curators can approve exact interpretation; website designer doesn't invent artistic meaning.

### This visitor experience is REALISTIC but not complete
Current source enables a factual archival flow. It **does not** yet license a media-led creative experience. That missing artist/film material is the actual production bottleneck, not lack of GSAP or Three.js.

## 3. Real staff/artist source packet — minimum to unblock one finished authored encounter

**Packet A — one film (Jusoor):**
Film title as authorized AR/EN, director public credit/bio, 1 approved still and 1 licensed clip or alternate static source, original resolution, credits, date/venue of real screening, 2–3 artist/curator-approved interpretive prompts, subtitles/captions/transcript, usage scope and expiry, source/contact approval.

**Packet B — one material work (Tales of Fustat or Natalia Ossef):**
Exact maker and work/process title, dated originals documenting a real sequence (not arbitrary adjacent scenes), high-res assets, photographer, maker/participant consent where relevant, material explanation, and source citation.

**Packet C — one spatial/installation work (INBETWEEN or Something Else):**
Artist, work title, installation year, actual venue, materials/scale, installation view and details in original high-res, photograph credit, artwork reproduction permissions, any authorized artist statement and spatial interaction scope.

**Institutional packet:** current official mark/vector, AR/EN programme/exhibition graphic originals and design credits, current venue/visit information and booking/open-call owner, staff-editor decision maker and CMS/hosting permissions.

**No fabricated requirements:** if artist doesn't want sound or video, a powerful static experience is valid. If current call isn't open, it stays archive-only. Technical style emerges from artistic packet.

## 4. How to validate before writing screens
Use *three real content walks* in a 20–30 minute Darb editorial workshop (duration just an optional workshop format, not delivery estimate):
- Film: editorial owner approves named film relationships and when an audiovisual layer is genuinely available.
- Material work: maker confirms one process chain and which documentation conveys its meaning.
- Installation: artist confirms spatial relation, crop/detail, accompanying text, exhibition identity treatment.

After approval build a **single integrated feature thread across entry, work, person, event, index and action**, then review actual mobile/desktop Vercel implementation. No A/B contest of homepage screenshots.
