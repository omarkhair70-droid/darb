# Stage 05 — Three visual research territories (source-image studies)

**2026-11-02 • NO WEBSITE CODE • NO FINAL DESIGN SELECTION**

Research boards were composed with **unaltered, credited-to-source** objects from the supplied Darb 1718 Overview 2026 PDF. The visual boards were delivered as three PNGs + a small ZIP in the associated ChatGPT research turn, using only existing project photos. **The research notes and every original image are in this repository; the separate montage PNGs are in the conversation delivery, not currently committed as binary files**.

All concepts below are **experiment labels and author-created working copy**, not official Darb slogans, quotes, or approved designs. Use each photo's asset number to retrieve the original from [assets/visual-audit-77.csv](../assets/visual-audit-77.csv) and [original source media](../assets/original/).

## 01 — BETWEEN / بين
**Question:** Can the experience reveal relationships **between the Fustat craft process, people, material, and contemporary art** without turning those people into a romantic postcard?

**Board composition:** Warm almost-neutral paper, generous editorial whitespace, large workshop photography paired with conversation and cyanotype objects; extremely restrained supporting type. Images used:
- **#07** (PDF p.3) — craft workers photographed making objects — source filename `assets/original/pdf_image_0323.jpeg`
- **#39** (PDF p.11) — group dialogue — `assets/original/pdf_image_0400.jpeg`
- **#41–42** (PDF p.11) — cyanotype print & materials — `pdf_image_0402.jpeg` and `pdf_image_0403.jpeg`
- **#01** (PDF p.1) — old Darb exterior, `pdf_image_0308.jpeg`

**Principle:** A photo alone is not the meaning; a sequence **maker → exchange → artifact → place** might unlock provenance. BUT these photographs are **different events**: the board **must not** imply they're one causal sequence.

**Visual choices only as test variables:** neutral warm canvas vs white gallery; serif editorial contrast vs contemporary Arabic sans; minimal linework / page indexing, no fake archaeological ornamental pattern or generic terracotta system.

**Better questions than “what's the color?”:** How do we avoid flattening different makers into anonymous hands? Do captions remain legible on small screens? Which artist's work has enough resolution/approval for inspection? Does this still look like Darb rather than a heritage tourism campaign?

**Anti-pattern:** treating the crafts and Fustat as a texture bank.

## 02 — MADE BY MANY / صُنع بأيدي كتير
**Question:** Can credit, conversation and collective activity become a **visual grammar of participation**, rather than 77 unrelated pictures of events?

**Board composition:** Dense dark charcoal reading surface, multiple documentary frames, repeated **credited event/source labels** rather than hero faces. Dark is **an experiment**, not an approved brand color.

Images used:
- **#03** (PDF p.2) — people in an exhibition/talk (`pdf_image_0314.jpeg`)
- **#31** (PDF p.9) — Jusoor film/audience discussion (`pdf_image_0381.jpeg`)
- **#38** (PDF p.10) — textile residency exchange (`pdf_image_0393.jpeg`)
- **#46** (PDF p.12) — collaborative writing/work (`pdf_image_0411.jpeg`)
- **#70–71** (PDF p.20) — a listening audience and live performance (`pdf_image_0469.jpeg`, `pdf_image_0470.jpeg`)

**Original evidence:** curator Power Ekroth's project *No Problem: The People*, part of Something Else 2026, names contributing behind-the-scenes labour and publicly includes Nourhan Elmeligy among contributors without specifying professional title: https://www.powerekroth.net/noproblemthepeople

**Experience candidate:** Credits as links. A photographer/technician/artist/participant (consenting and named) can become an entrypoint into projects, sounds and places. When credit is missing, show an editorial review gap, not a fictional biography.

**Anti-pattern:** sentimental language about anonymous workers and stylized animations of real faces; press imagery should not be mistaken for a signed collaboration narrative.

## 03 — WHAT THEN? / وماذا بعد؟
**Question:** Can genuine curatorial inquiry turn an archive into a living unfinished sequence instead of a “demolition and rebirth” trailer?

**Board composition:** Very wide photographic scale, austere editorial whitespace, artwork/site references juxtaposed; an **experimental isolated warm accent** (not a permanent Darb color).

Images used:
- **#58** (PDF p.17) — 2023 Something Else Citadel audience and architecture (`pdf_image_0445.png`)
- **#62** (PDF p.17) — suspended artwork in historic hall (`pdf_image_0449.png`)
- **#66** (PDF p.18) — historic building in evening (`pdf_image_0457.jpeg`)
- **#69** (PDF p.19) — installation (`pdf_image_0465.jpeg`)
- **#21** (PDF p.7) — INBETWEEN 2025 installation (`pdf_image_0359.jpeg`)

**Original evidence:** organizer's overview p.18 states Something Else 2023 theme *What Then?* (linked to W. B. Yeats), with chief curator Simon Njami, artistic director Moataz Nasr. The concept asks “what after?” **It is not a currently verified Darb institution slogan**.

**Attention rule:** the 2024 physical demolition is historically documented elsewhere, but there are **no verified 2024 demolition photographs in this 77-photo project source**. Don't pretend the Citadel photos document demolition or assign #66 to the wrong building.

**Anti-pattern:** gratuitous glitch, falling walls, 3D rubble, a “reborn” arc presented as fact.

## Three boards are NOT three color themes
These studies test: image authorship & resolution, photography density and hierarchy, Arabic/Latin typographic relationship, space for artwork, motion necessity, emotional tone and routes to current programmes.

### Candidate evaluation matrix (test, not scored yet)
| Criterion | Method |
|---|---|
| Distinctly Darb | Blind test: can viewer identify this as Darb rather than a generic contemporary art centre? |
| Source fidelity | Are every image/event/name/context accurate? |
| Art authorship | Do works retain integrity, correct crop, credit and permission? |
| Arabic/English equivalence | Arabic gets designed rhythm, not copied Latin positions |
| Cultural utility | Find current workshop / open call / visit/contact within 2 interactions |
| Originality vs fashion | Remove animations: does the composition still communicate? |
| Emotional intelligence | Convey continuity, not a made-up heroic rescue |
| Performance | Existing PDF photographs may require original hires images for large image backgrounds |

### Current status
**All three pass source-image viability as research collages. None is approved, scorecard results are not measured, and no brand palette or animation stack was locked.** Next: curatorial captions and image permissions, audience/artist review, then two independent **bilingual typography & page rhythm experiments** based on the winning *principle*, not a copied layout.
