# Visual grammar laboratory — no locked brand identity yet

Start with Darb's material. Do not choose colors by browsing Awwwards.

## Material observations from 2026 PDF
- **Fustat / ceramics:** clay dust, workshop marks, glaze, handmade irregularities (pp. 1, 3, 11).
- **Architecture and circulation:** brick, courtyard, mural, gallery dark/light, artist-made plans (pp. 1, 4, 21).
- **Paint and print:** original artworks, wheat-paper surfaces, dark-room/cyanotype blue from Tales of Fustat (pp. 4, 11).
- **Polyphonic spaces:** audiences seated informally, live performance, photography, group dialogues (pp. 3, 9, 12, 20).
- **Global / local art:** Citadel installations, Something Else international programme, craft residency (pp. 10, 15–19).

## Visual hypotheses to test
1. **Editorial openness:** unusually generous white/empty space with image-first arrangements — let exhibits breathe, not 12 equal cards.
2. **Uneven rhythm:** asymmetric composition reflects variety of practices but obeys readable responsive grids.
3. **Art-author first:** dynamic color accents sampled from *artworks* only with contextual ownership; no forced branded overlay on art.
4. **Printed memory:** carefully controlled paper edge, silkscreen artifacts, pencil marks, film/grain **only when a document/material connection justifies it**.
5. **Bilingual type as authorship:** Arabic display + Arabic body + Latin display/body matched in weight, rhythm, glyph clarity. Test numeral conventions, bidirectional captions and mixed scripts.
6. **Space is sound capable but silent by default:** ambience belongs to a project, never automatic across the entire site.
7. **Calm motion as choreography:** proximity → reveal → focus → stillness; no constant parallax.

## Experiments
- Type: 3 contrast pairs (quiet editorial / experimental exhibition / documentary documentary), test with long Arabic titles and dates.
- Grid: 2 editorial templates, 1 immersive image-led template, 1 densely searchable archive template.
- Media treatments: full-bleed cropped photo vs accurately framed art vs object detail zoom.
- Navigation: index led, magazine led, graph led; keyboard + touch studies.
- Motion: no-motion reference still needs composition; then add justified movement.
- Content states: missing media, no artist biography, event canceled, translated/untranslated entry.

## What NOT to copy
Nordiska museum's burgundy and shape mask; Rijksmuseum's exact magazine template; Oculi Mundi's celestial sphere; Getty's animation timings; Imaginarium's floating paintings or AI reinterpretations.

## Evidence-based identity questions
Could the identity be built around **an encounter** rather than around a named object? Would Darb prefer a consistent institutional system or an elastic exhibitor-authored one? Which archival surfaces have approval and clear authorship? Should site typography be closer to contemporary Arab editorial typography than global generic grotesks? Confirm via client.
