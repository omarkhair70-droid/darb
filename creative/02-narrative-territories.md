# Story territories — directions to TEST, not final designs

## Territory A — The Living Archive
**Thesis:** The archive is alive because people keep using and revising it.

- Opening: one genuine physical detail (image or short edited footage), accompanied by **current activity**, not a generic “welcome” paragraph.
- Memory: artworks, people, sound and place linked like an editorial constellation; allow the viewer to enter from multiple points.
- Interaction: selecting a story brings up artifacts/works/people related to it; time is contextual, not just pagination.
- Primary data: 2008 mission; 2023 Something Else; Tales of Fustat, Jusoor, Natalia residency, UNSPOKEN; 2025–26 events.
- Risks: graph becomes inscrutable; requires accurate entity mapping, editorial stewardship, accessible list fallback.
- Proof prototype: choose one exhibition and navigate **artwork → artist → programme → venue → next event**.

## Territory B — A Space That Moves
**Thesis:** A cultural space is more than a building, but architecture and loss are real.

- Opening: architectural surface and its human use; *not* a fabricated walking tour.
- Story shifts: founding in Fustat → intersecting practices → pressure / demolition 2023–24 → projects and gatherings 2025–26 → unfinished future.
- Motion can use **material transformations** — stage/courtyard/photos/print scraps appearing/disappearing, never simulated demolition for shock.
- Could integrate 2026 overview floor sketches as documents **only if client confirms what they depict**.
- Risks: one dramatic setback overshadows artists and current work; sensitive reporting must stay attributed.
- Proof prototype: 90-second optional scroll story that returns to current programmes, with skip and accessible text.

## Territory C — The Act of Gathering
**Thesis:** Darb is not the artist or the gallery; it is what happens when people meet.

- Enter through plural voices: film discussion, hands at clay workshop, audience at performance, group residency, communal exhibition.
- Use original documentary photos with names/captions; multiple perspectives, non-linear entry paths.
- Intentional audio: rooms can have discrete field-recorded ambience with opt-in consent; never a continuous intrusive soundtrack.
- Events and workshops connected to knowledge and work archive, making practical and artistic content one system.
- Risks: lack of participant consent/identification; people cannot be aesthetic props.
- Proof prototype: a mobile-first programme-to-people story with direct registration and archive context.

## Three rival hypotheses to test
- H1: Story-first landing raises interest but slows programme discovery.
- H2: A living-archive graph builds depth but overwhelms new visitors.
- H3: Photo-led, editorial modular layout has stronger impact than a full 3D environment for Darb's authentic documentary collection.

## Direction choice gate
Present 3 reference boards and 3 narrative sample sequences using **actual** named Darb images. Choose based on goals, source availability, stakeholder response, user tests, mobile clarity and preservation of the cultural mission — not award-chasing.
