# Stage 04 — Three story prototypes on paper (NO UI/code)

**Date 2 Nov 2026.** These are three **rival** narrative storyboards grounded in the 21-page Darb 1718 Overview 2026 and image IDs in [77-asset audit](../assets/visual-audit-77.md). These are **hypotheses for critique**, not final website routes, scripts, animation decisions, or selected direction.

**Non-negotiable:** The homepage must reveal real present programmes and a path to events immediately; stories are an invitation, never a required cinematic interstitial. Arabic and English both require editorial direction. Exhibition art is not anonymous decorative texture. Photos include named/unnamed people and works, so captions, identities, photographer credits, venue/date and publication rights need client review.

---

## Concept A — BETWEEN / المسافة اللي بتجمعنا

**Provocation:** What makes a space a cultural space? Not its walls alone — the new encounters it makes possible.

**Source truth:** Darb was founded in Old Cairo/Fustat to bring local craft practices and experimental contemporary arts together (overview p.2); the 2025–26 activities prove diversity in how it is used. Its main building was demolished in 2024, but programmes continued (press and overview). That's a *factual continuity*, not an invented complete rebirth.

| Beat | Material/image | Emotional/reader movement | Editorial interaction hypothesis | Context/rights gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Threshold | **#01 exterior**, 2026 PDF p.1 | A distinctive real place, not a generic gallery | Stable first screen showing real event now + one open story invitation | Exact date of image and current venue state |
| 02 — Hands | **#07 pottery**, **#35 textile process** | Physical acts of making | Intercut material close details and short labelled maker context | Maker names and credit; never imply these photos all depict same event |
| 03 — Encounter | **#39 interviews**, **#40 group**, p.11 | Listening as collaboration | Select a phrase or documented story to open its source/photo | Workshop names, permission to quote/interview |
| 04 — Impression | **#41, #42 cyanotypes**, p.11 | Photography becomes an object made by a person | Quiet inspection/zoom with maker caption | Confirm work ownership; original scans for zoom |
| 05 — Other Rooms | **#30 screenings**, **#46 writing**, **#70 audience** | One cultural practice opens into many | Related-programme links, not endless carousel | Events not conflated; captions separately |
| 06 — Continue | **#21 installation** or current approved photo, pp.7/2026 | Today's work is ongoing | Return to actual What’s On, artist stories and join/collaborate | Current programming, status and locations |

**Art-direction experiment:** high-contrast real material (clay, cyanotype blue, paper) with an unusually restrained reading interface; art objects keep their real colors. Use asymmetry and spare paper space only when supporting attention. No automatic palette from clay; no fake “vintage Egypt” background.

**Motion hypothesis:** texture/frame change as conceptual *transformation of process* (e.g. photo to cyanotype), but must not pretend the images record one chronological sequence. Static editorial layout must work without motion.

**Proof test:** a first-time visitor can say (1) what Darb is, (2) how contemporary artists meet Fustat craftspeople, (3) where to find actual programmes, after 90 seconds.

**Potential failure:** romanticizing craftspeople as atmosphere rather than credited co-authors.

## Concept B — MADE BY MANY / الشغل اللي محدش بيشوفه

**Provocation:** An exhibition is never made by the artists alone.

**Primary documentary reference:** Curator Power Ekroth's *No Problem: The People* (Cairo 2026), explicitly foregrounds technicians, caretakers, managers, administrators, neighbours, fabricators and shared effort, and publicly credits Nourhan Elmeligy among contributors. It does not define Nourhan's exact title. https://www.powerekroth.net/noproblemthepeople

| Beat | Material/image | Emotional/reader movement | Interactive hypothesis | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Enter a shared room | **#03 seated audience** or **#70 concert listeners** | Everyone is a participant, not just spectator | Frame photo as observed gathering; caption-first | Names/event and photo consent |
| 02 — Invisible work | **#38 textile discussion** / **#39 interviews** | From finished artwork to people who made it possible | Optional link to attributed craftsperson/technician/organizer | Never invent who did what |
| 03 — Voices | **#31 Jusoor discussion** / **#33 filmmakers in conversation** | Film travels through a room, not through interface alone | Discussion transcript/audio only if real and approved | Film rights and speaker names |
| 04 — Co-authorship | **#43–46 writing workshop** | Collaboration is both process and outcome | Group work shown with protected participant identities | Participant consent |
| 05 — A wider circle | **#53–55 Something Else symposium**, **#58/67 Citadel visitors** | Local practice and international network | Contextual relations by project/curator/artist, not fabricated network | All curator/artist IDs sourced |
| 06 — A credit becomes navigation | **#41 printed object**, names as approved | Credit is a way into next story | Person → project → artwork → programme linked graph | Role/biography approval |
| 07 — Open invitation | Current verified programme | Culture continues through people | Direct ‘Attend / Join / Collaborate’ | Actual action URLs |

**Art-direction experiment:** typography carries contributors with serious, generous credits. Documentary photographic collage *can* be layered but can't blur evidence or alter art; visual rhythm comes from different formats and voices. Story is polyphonic, not founder versus adversity.

**Motion:** small relational transitions show connections; don't animate names of real people in a way that suggests a quote or involvement not documented.

**Proof test:** after experience, users can name at least three *different kinds of labour* Darb supports, and navigate to a specific upcoming programme.

**Potential failure:** many biographies missing and several participants have privacy constraints. Need careful fact collection and opt-in.

## Concept C — WHAT THEN? / وبعد كده؟

**Provocation:** Art is a question that never finishes. Darb moves through shifts, not simply from tragedy to triumph.

**Documented origin:** Something Else III (2023, Citadel) explicitly used W. B. Yeats's question **“What Then?”**; the organiser's overview lists 22 curators, 171 artists and 42 countries. This provides a real curatorial prompt, not an invented tagline. The centre's 2023–24 disruption and subsequent programming may form a secondary context only when sourced.

| Beat | Material/image | Reader movement | Editorial hypothesis | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — A question | **#58/67 Citadel wide views** | Open physical scale, unanswered question | Readers can choose ‘Work’, ‘People’ or ‘Place’ | Show *What Then?* as an artwork's curatorial question, not Darb's slogan |
| 02 — Many answers | **#56, #57, #61–63 artworks** | Differing media and perspectives | Curated artworks encountered one by one with credits | Curator/artist/country details |
| 03 — The site as an active condition | **#66 historical facade** | Place and its memory | Place-caption toggles past/present with evidence | Verify whether #66 location is Darb or Citadel; no wrong building ID |
| 04 — Rupture, reported | **no image from supplied 77 establishes the 2024 demolition** | Context of loss without spectacle | Brief attributed timeline and sourced quote/press photo only if cleared | 2024 source media, chronology, stakeholder approval |
| 05 — More than survival | **#21 (INBETWEEN), #26 (Observer Speaks), #18 (UNSPOKEN), #70 (performance)** | Creative work keeps changing form | Nonlinear timeline routes to individual projects | No blending unrelated events into one |
| 06 — What can happen next? | verified 2026 programme | Unfinished future / participation | Direct calendar, residency, artist inquiry | Confirm currently active channel |

**Art-direction experiment:** the material itself drives composition; moments of stillness amid wildly diverse media. Potential editorial contrast of scale and almost-blank white rather than dramatized rubble, glitch, huge sans-serif slogans, or fake damaged typography.

**Motion:** an archival relation can reveal what came after; no virtual demolition scene or shock camera move.

**Proof test:** users distinguish Something Else's own art history from Darb's physical crisis and from current work.

**Potential failure:** the 2024 building story eclipses the independent 2025–26 programme. Need make present art dominant.
