## Precise approvals to obtain

### From filmmaker / producing institution
- Preferred official full film credit wording AR and EN: director/researcher, producers, camera, sound, editing, subtitles, participants.
- Confirmation that published YouTube version is the approved full or public screening version; standard embed permitted on a **client cultural organization website**? If no, official link only.
- If a bespoke cinematic experience is desired, *optional* licensed media packet: original 1080p or higher short excerpt, 2–3 original stills/poster masters, timecodes, copyright owner, crop/manipulation rules and territorial/duration scope.
- Filmmaker-approved thematic interpretation or 2–3 genuine viewing/listening prompts tied to exact scenes/timecodes; no fabricated quotes.
- Subtitle language files/caption transcript or approved accessible alternative; identify who holds subtitle translation rights.
- Names/consent of anyone whose face/voice is individually highlighted beyond original published film (especially workshop close-ups), if separately reused.

### From Darb 1718 curatorial/operations
- Jusoor actual programme title AR/EN and original exhibition/film-identity artwork + design credits.
- Confirm the specific Braids of Identity screening event date, venue, format, filmmaker attendance or Q&A **only if documented**; archive materials and agreed wording.
- Confirm which current film submission/open call URLs are open now (if any), with deadlines and actual verified venue/status. Historic source links must not appear as open booking actions.
- Official Darb identity/vector artwork and curator/editor who signs off on film/context/people nodes and the final artistic presentation.

## The only questions to put to a creative reviewer before building the art scene
1. **What is the film's authored invitation?** Which particular act/time/voice does the filmmaker want us to help a visitor look at attentively, and why?
2. **How much can the visitor navigate and change?** Curated scene points, captions, transcript annotations, or only the intact original film? Artist controls this decision.
3. **What relationship to Jusoor is important?** A specific screening/discussion vs part of a broader film programme, with source evidence.
4. **What should not be reused or cropped?** Rights and the integrity of women/participants' representation, especially in the context of displacement and living heritage.

## No premature visual/technical commitment
- No borrowed Fokhara layout, black minimalist movie-player template, fake floating braid strands, fake handmade textures, artificial animations, cinematic soundscape or interactive quiz.
- The original film must remain accessible as a complete intact viewer/source. Optional authored interactions can augment but not overwrite it.
- A film-focused experience must connect to the director, the original Jusoor programme, other correctly credited films and usable Darb routes. This is a whole-site vertical slice, not a standalone promotional landing page.
- No promises of feature completeness or rights clearance until written source approvals are recorded.
