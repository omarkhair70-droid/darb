# Darb 1718 — Creative & Experience Brief v0.1

**Working proposition:** An internationally legible, artistically distinctive bilingual website that is an **active cultural space and living editorial archive**, not an institutional brochure or memorial.

## Reality / why
Darb 1718 connects contemporary art, local artisans, experimental performance, film, international residencies, learning and civic participation in Fustat/Old Cairo (official overview, pp. 1–13). The past includes physical threat and partial demolition in 2024, alongside renewed programmes visible in the 2025/26 overview. Do not reduce identity to victimhood: today's work matters independently of the crisis.

## Working insight
The most distinctive material is not one image; it is **the relations among artworks, people, place and time**. A photograph of a potter in Fustat, a film conversation, a contemporary installation and a collaborative exhibition are parts of one cultural ecology.

## Main user problem — research hypothesis
International curators and first-time visitors might know the name or a viral news story but not the extent, openness, current status or continuity of the programme. Local audiences may need direct, reliable event/workshop information, registration and a way to encounter artists. **Validate with staff/audience interviews.**

## What the website must do
1. Let a new visitor understand what Darb **is today** within seconds — without a dramatic intro barrier.
2. Reward deeper exploration through stories, artists, programmes and place.
3. Preserve and credit past work without building an unmanageable database.
4. Make current/upcoming activity, calls, venue and collaboration paths usable.
5. Support bilingual Arabic RTL and English LTR equally; never make Arabic an afterthought.
6. Offer genuinely optional immersive moments, grounded in actual source media.
7. Be maintainable by a small programme team.

## Audience jobs
- **New international visitor:** understand artistic identity, browse significant projects, contact partnerships.
- **Local cultural visitor:** what's on, where/when, register and share.
- **Emerging artist:** opportunities, artist services, open calls, apply.
- **Artist/researcher:** chronology, previous exhibitions, credits, image/context archive.
- **Partner/donor/press:** credible impact, official contact and downloadable press/assets.
- **Staff/editor:** confidently publish and update without developer dependency.

## Non-goals
- A landing-only Awwwards demonstration, spinning permanent 3D art gallery, copied western museum art direction, overlong manifesto, 2024-only “disaster” story, fabricated testimonial, or production code prior to research gate.

## Narrative tone
Assured, experimental, generous; bilingual and contemporary without “ancient Egypt” clichés or exoticization. Source-led. Give artists' work room to speak; use silence and white/negative space when valuable. Dramatic **because real**, not because copy exaggerates.

## Open decisions, NOT approvals
- What proportion of homepage should be story vs what’s on?
- Are events/registration/residency submissions managed externally?
- Should visitors explore **by story, year, discipline, person, or event**?
- Which story has image/consent rights and accurate participant credits?
- Are donations, space hire, press pack, membership, public API required?
- Confirm post-2024 visitor venue/address and schedule via Darb team.

## Definition of success (proposed measurable signals)
Programme discovery, registration completion, artist page engagement, archive exploration beyond homepage, partner enquiries, CMS publishing time, mobile LCP/INP/CLS, accessibility audits. No invented performance or donation metrics.

**Input materials:** uploaded Overview 2026 (21pp, 77 visual objects), 2008–2026 chronology, first-party social posts and independent reportage.
