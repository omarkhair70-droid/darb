# Overview 2026 — 21-page reference transcript and scene notes

This content is a descriptive research index extracted from the supplied 21-page PDF. It is **not a replacement for the layout and images in the PDF**.

1. **Cover:** exterior mural, Darb 1718 logo, tree / courtyard.
2. **About / mission:** Old Cairo center, established 2008, emerging artists, public access, creative confidence, civic/cross-cultural exchange. Workshops, residencies, screenings, performances, local craftspeople.
3. **Format overview:** exhibitions/artist residencies; concerts/film screenings; community outreach; workshops/local artisans.
4. **Exhibitions:** multi-photo gallery, visitors, artworks, experiments including VR.
5. **UNSPOKEN / ما لم يُقال:** Sep 12–25 2026, exhibition on art, healing, self-expression and rehabilitation; works made in art-therapy sessions at Gamal Abu Al Azayem Hospital.
6. **Darb fi Al-Hadiqqa / A Path in the Garden:** Nov 25–Dec 14 2025; mixed-media group exhibition 17 artists, 2 workshops.
7. **INBETWEEN: Thin Places, Movements, and Meetings:** May 22–Jun 4 2025; installation and photographic art.
8. **مش مجرد عيال / Observer Speaks: A Young Artists' Guide to the World:** Aug 16–23 2025; emerging voices, multi-artwork exhibition.
9. **Jusoor / جسور:** short films, public discussion; Sudanese filmmaker films *Dukhan*, *Braids of Identity*, *The Last Henna*, *Motive*; Iranian films in collaboration with Left Bank Cinema Collective.
10. **Natalia Ossef residency:** textiles, Ramses Wissa Wassef Centre, khayameya in Bab Zuweila, Tunis Village in Fayoum, concluding open studio.
11. **Tales of Fustat:** photowalk, potters, portraits, storytelling; photographic cyanotype workshops and printed notebooks.
12. **A Room of One's Own / غرفة تخصنا وحدنا:** feminist writer/researcher Aisha Aljaedy, four weeks of collective writing about space/home/gender/boundaries.
13. **Voice in Motion / الحركة في الصوت:** voice as group play and expression, improvisation, movement/listening.
14. **Alexandria Biennale:** 12-year break, *This Too Shall Pass*, curated by Moataz Nasr; sector context, not an in-house Darb event.
15. **Something Else:** international contemporary visual arts event developed alongside later independent of the official Cairo Biennale; editions 2015, 2018, 2023, 2025 symposium, 2026 Bayt Al Sinnari.
16. **Something Else Symposium:** Feb 8–22 2025, Darb 1718 and Bayt Al Sinnari.
17. **Something Else at the Citadel:** Nov 2023 installations, images and historic fortress context.
18. **Something Else III:** Nov 23–Dec 23 2023; 22 curators, 171 artists, 42 countries; W. B. Yeats *What Then?*, chief curator Simon Njami, artistic director Moataz Nasr, guest Ahmed Nawwar.
19. **Citadel archive photos:** wide public setting, live music/performance, installations.
20. **Archive photos:** live musical performance and listening in the hall.
21. **Plans:** outdoor, first floor, second floor; credited to Rokaya Abdelhafez. Do not assume exact measured construction.
