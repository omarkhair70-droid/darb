**Method:** inspected the contact sheet created from all **77 individual embedded PDF assets** across 21 pages, crosschecked with page topics. Image titles below are **visual descriptions**, not verified museum captions or attributions. No identities are inferred from faces.

**Priority guide:** `H` = promising original story/feature asset; `M` = supporting/needs context; `L` = archival/reference only or insufficient for hero; `X` = layout noise not to publish as image. *Priority is not license clearance, photographic quality, or an art-history valuation.*

**Important findings:** The 77 objects include **three plans (#75–77)**, **repeat logo variants (#2, #8, #9, #72)**, **graphic title designs (#14, #29)** and **two layout fragments (#73–74)**. So **77 ≠ 77 independent photographic stories**. Pages 5 (UNSPOKEN) and 12 (workshop) call for heightened care on participants, named credits and consent. Many image objects are under 1000px wide; don't plan unbounded fullscreen print-quality display solely from PDF images.
