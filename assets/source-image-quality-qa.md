## Production risks and requests
1. Request **original photographs** for hero and close-zoom, not compressed PDF copies; resolution and sharpness may limit large-screen presentation. Current source has just six assets >=1600px wide including one plan.
2. Request original photograph shot/date/location; art title; artist; curator; photographer and usage rights; individual portrait/participant consent where required.
3. Keep artwork reproductions visually faithful: no arbitrary overlaid type or crop that distorts the work; provide dedicated art detail/view page with credits.
4. Build responsive crop/art-direction variants **only after** event/artist identification and approval.
5. With `UNSPOKEN` (#14–18), focus on exhibited works and publicly approved programme description, without attaching clinical diagnoses or confidential health status to individual photographs.
6. Test print-like textures carefully: can derive from authentic cyanotype/process, but avoid using distressed paper as generic grief trope.
7. Clarify difference between historical 2023 venue, current 2026 venue and proposed plans on page 21.

## Non-goals
No arbitrary AI enhancement/upscaling or invented image provenance. A visual planning sheet is **not** a rights-cleared final asset bank. No website code.
