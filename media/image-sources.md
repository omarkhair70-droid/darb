# Public photo & video source registry

**This is an inventory of reference links, not a redistribution of copyrighted photos.** Rights often belong to photographer, institution or publisher. Record credit, licence, context, capture date, and permission before use in a public website or paid campaign.

## Original uploaded PDF archive
The commissioner's 21-page *Darb 1718 Overview 2026* PDF contains **77 embedded image assets / appearances** (verified by local PDF extraction). Locally generated research bundle includes original PDF, page previews, an image manifest CSV and all embedded image assets. **The PDF is not publicly redistributed by this repo.** See [source overview](../source-material/overview-2026.md).

### Recommended editorial image groupings
1. **Place before demolition:** street entrance, courtyard, gallery, rooftop; historical-only.
2. **Rupture:** dated 2023 warnings and 2024 demolition journalism; avoid conflating images of different dates.
3. **Collective support:** artists, supporters, 2025 fundraiser and programme.
4. **Work & makers:** pottery, artisans, artist residencies and cyanotype.
5. **Re-emergence:** 2025–2026 exhibition installation, audience and programme artwork.
6. **Something Else:** 2015/2018/2023/2025/2026 separately labeled.

## Research guardrails
- Do not use an *artist's work* as if it were Darb's brand asset.
- Never present the old address/venue as unquestionably open today.
- Save source URL and copyright holder before visual selection.
- Photos of therapeutic or marginalized groups need extra consent and context.
- PDF page previews are best treated as internal reference until explicit sharing rights are checked.
