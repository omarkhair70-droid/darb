## A Living Archive Whose Identity Emerges From Its Connections

**Status:** New reference research and art-direction proposal; **not an approved redesign or implemented scene**.  
**Input:** The owner’s latest screenshots (black floating circular menu, Oculi Mundi collection sphere and floating artwork field), earlier Getty screen recording, and owner’s original proposal: a **spiderweb-like network whose ends reveal the letters D, A, R, B**, continuing into the archive.

## What the latest screenshots expose

### The white screen with the circular black control
- A single high-contrast dark circular button has more visual weight than the faint atmosphere behind it.
- Its presence is not the main problem. What it **opens** matters: a conventional category list would negate the spatial logic we are trying to build.
- Entry/wayfinding should be a transformation *inside the same archive*, not a new overlay masquerading as spatial navigation.

### Oculi screenshot A — collection globe
- The collector’s worlds appear as distinct volumetric objects, with individual original maps on a 3D spherical surface.
- A category is **a place one can approach**; not a link in a header.
- Orbiting maps and surrounding constellations create a sense of scale and discovery.

### Oculi screenshot B — floating collection
- Works occupy foreground, middle-distance and background in a dark void.
- The scene is not a rigid gallery grid. The visitor can perceive hundreds of objects without reading a traditional list.
- The spare controls at the bottom maintain orientation without hijacking the view.

**Crucial distinction:** We must not simply make a map globe with Darb images pasted to it. Darb is about artistic practices, collaborations, communities, exhibitions and the material transformation of memory; its own visual laws need to emerge from these facts.

## Darb-specific central proposal: THE WOVEN NAME / الاسم المنسوج

**One evolving direction, not three unrelated homepage pitches.**

### Thesis
> **DARB is not a word placed over its archive. DARB is a pattern formed by the encounters inside its archive.**

The starting frame is a living constellation of genuine archival traces. Fine, tensioned filaments connect source objects according to distinct relationship types:
1. **Historically documented connections** (event–work, person–programme, place–event).
2. **Editorial/curatorial parallels** across different projects (clearly identified as interpretations).
3. **Visual echoes** (color, technique, shape), explicitly presented as aesthetic, not historical causation.

The visitor's arrival activates a handful of filaments. Four major knots at spatially separated coordinates first suggest four different gestures: `D`, `A`, `R`, `B`. Each letter is a transient **camera-readable projection of real strands**; rotate the world and it ceases to be a letter and becomes an archive of people, works, materials and projects. The letterforms are never floating labels added after the fact.

**Note:** We have no reason to assign one mandatory subject category to each letter. Avoid a fake acronym (D = discover, A = art, etc.). Let the letters become spatial viewpoints and four ways into *one* world rather than four website sections.

### Spatial sequence / movement score
**A. Before the name — 0–? visitor-driven**  
Blankness is a real pause, not a loading screen. A first photo/object appears in true archival material. Nearby threads appear in response to focus, not automatically as a gimmick.

**B. The first knot**  
A controlled spatial move exposes an initial branching network. Text is tiny and specific: the place, work, date, or practice that supports the connection. No large conventional hero heading.

**C. A letter occurs**  
As the camera moves to a particular viewing angle, several lines/archival fragments align into `D` for a brief moment. Not a perfect printed font; a legible emergent glyph with small irregularities. The reading should feel discovered, not announced.

**D. The glyph dissolves into a route**  
The visitor chooses a thread. Lines stretch into depth, paper/photo/media artifacts take their own paths, and a real source object becomes fully inspectable. The next letters are possible discoveries, not mandatory chapters.

**E. From artwork to different artwork**  
An artwork's medium governs its encounter: cyanotype can foreground blue print/paper grain; photography can foreground framing, room and community context; a performance can foreground durational media if present; installation can foreground site and space. No generic modal or same zoom easing for all.

**F. Re-emergence**  
Pulling back can reassemble the archive from a *different* angle: one long storyline may leave the viewer with a different glyph, revealing the four-letter identity as a network of many stories.

### Four distinct but connected systems
- **A spatial collection field:** grounded in real, correct media; room for irregular scale, depth and context.
- **A relationship field:** authored filaments with semantic edge types, directional camera travel and evidence access.
- **A generative identity:** D-A-R-B emerges at purposeful viewpoints and can be read even in still media.
- **A sonic layer:** audio proximity and negative space, with sound off by default until explicitly enabled. Sonic signatures derive from artistic material, never an arbitrary continuous ambient loop.

## Material-specific encounter language (the next quality leap)

| Media | Potential visual behavior | Potential sonic behavior (original audio only) | What must be avoided |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyanotype prints | Paper-like unfurl, blue/white material exposure, detail held at supported resolution | dry paper/fiber texture; no forced sound | blue overlay on all images |
| Documentary photography | Subject remains framed; nearby project objects recede; context can re-enter | silence or authentic ambient sound if available | artificial face animation |
| Textiles/crafts | Strands and woven layout can introduce visible relationships | textile/handling textures if purpose-built | fake 3D sculpted replicas |
| Installations | Changing spatial camera exposes actual documented environment | room acoustics if legitimately recorded | pretending a photograph is a scan |
| Performance/video | Duration and rhythm become the primary dimension | original licensed performance recording | generic background music replacement |
| Printed notebooks | Pages/folio movement controlled by user, at real scan quality | restrained tactile folio/page sound | copied Oculi book carousel |

## Award-worthy production discipline
- A distinctive Darb-specific conceptual thesis; no pastiche of Getty/Oculi.
- 1 complete premium `object → relationship → artwork → retreat` slice before spreading any generic effect to all 72 objects.
- Build a separate **identity lab** for the D-A-R-B filament projection with actual archive nodes. It must pass a legibility test with and without motion.
- Browser video review at 1440 and 390 widths; checks of motion rhythm, image-edge treatment, typography during movement, scroll/drag interruptions and return state.
- Sound tests opt-in, with a physically informed pause/rest beat. All paths retain quiet and accessible representations.
- Profile image decoding, GPU/CPU allocations, texture loading and frame pacing; reject spectacle that performs only on a powerful laptop.
- Exhibition variant later: kiosk/fullscreen installation with scalable input, optional speakers, a gallery-safe 3-minute loop **only when nobody is interacting**, clear credits and user-led paths. This is a planned output, not current functionality.
- Preserve both English and Arabic as authored spatial layouts.
- Treat awards as possible submissions, not promised recognition.
