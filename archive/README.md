## Stage 08 — Cloth-first entrance and direct-source passage

- Three **actual textile eye positions** are now direct touch/pointer/keyboard entrances, using the same source records 39 / 41 / 42. Each has a discoverable, low-chrome focus ring; their semantic labels remain available to assistive technology.
- The visitor can also **continue scrolling or pressing Arrow/Space** once the fabric is open. This follows one continuous default witness thread unless the visitor intentionally focuses another eye. A three-option choice is never forced.
- Entry grows a viewport portal from the chosen physical aperture using its actual source image. The existing archive camera starts moving toward that same record underneath the cloth, with interface chrome deferred until after visual arrival.
- The woven optional sound remains off until explicitly enabled, exposed as a tiny button with a clear accessible label rather than a permanent **SOUND ON/OFF** status panel.
- AR/EN, immediate skip and reduced-motion system settings remain supported. The previously accepted archive remains unaltered at \`/\` while Stage 08 receives creative review.
- Desktop/mobile browser tests now validate real eye selection, continued scroll entry, absence of hero/debug UI and full passage video.

**Still not final:** commissioned material quality, truly volumetric cloth behavior and eye geometry, detailed moment-to-moment color grading, audio mix, polished exhibition ending, and fully accepted creative sequence require additional authored review. ## Stage 05 — Archive Compass (same project; replacing the old menu index)

The earlier black circular menu opened a conventional **opaque search-and-chapter-list page**. That contradicted the continuous archive design even though the spatial world behind it worked.

Stage 05 changes the interaction itself:
- The circular menu now **pulls the camera back from the current artwork into a source-image atlas**, while a translucent constellation comes into focus inside the **same** spatial experience.
- All **17 source-programme clusters** appear as spatial waypoints, connected by the existing **nine clearly identified editorial thematic relationships**, not an alphabetical menu or chapter list.
- Highlight/focus on any cluster shows its representative real archival image and the actual source pages; selecting **Enter This Thread** sends the camera into that cluster.
- Free text search across source objects remains available but appears as a contained, contextual instrument rather than a separate website section.
- Closing the compass returns to the prior camera target, mode, detail state and scroll position. Source-object encounters can be resumed.
- A keyboard-focusable atlas, AR/EN context and touch-scrollable mobile map preserve alternative ways of navigating.
- GitHub Actions Playwright checks assert that the menu produces a constellation, that entry leads back into the world, and that the previous chapter-list layout does not return.

This is one concrete interaction correction, **not** a new homepage, public release, or a claim that all motion, visual, sound and exhibition work is complete.

### Stage 04 — The Woven D / Object Encounter (iterating in the **same** archive)

- One **D-shaped spatial filament** formed from real source images #39–42 from Tales of Fustat; it is a first working letter, not the completed D-A-R-B identity.

- Documentary photography and cyanotype use different environment tones; nonselected works recede, while relevant programme and curated cross-programme paths remain visible.

- The camera now interpolates based on elapsed time, not per-frame assumptions, so slow-frame-rate devices don't cause indefinitely prolonged moves.
- Source names, filament knots and encounter labels support AR/EN. A text-first reading alternative and reduced-motion controls remain.
- Playwright tests require an actual selected source image to occupy the viewport and test first D entry → cyanotype → curated relationship → retrace → archive on desktop and mobile.

This is an iterative high-end creative slice, not an assertion of Getty/Oculi final quality, award acceptance, or a finished audio installation. Complete visual review and browser QA on each new slice before adding further letters and the sound layer.

## Stage 03 / Source Atlas, 9 thematic threads, 16 spatial narrative moments

A standalone cultural exploration built from Darb 1718's supplied 2026 institutional overview. Not the older rejected Darb corporate-site experiment. **This is an iterative creative build, not an announcement of final Getty-level quality.**

## What changed since the Getty screen-recording review

- One permanent 9,200 × 6,400 source-led world, composed from genuine archival media rather than a conventional header/hero/sections workflow.
- **77 original PDF source records** indexed in \`data/media-catalog.json\`, with exact PDF page, XREF, source filename, editorial kind, programme and Arabic/English labels.
- **72 positioned archive objects** in \`src/world-layout.mjs\`, spanning **17 programme/context clusters**. Five non-editorial layout fragments / repeated logos stay in the source index but do not clutter the stage.
- **16 authored camera viewpoints** in \`data/world-chapters.json\`: move continuously between exhibitions, crafts, residencies, Jusoor, INBETWEEN, workshops, performances, and Tales of Fustat.
- **Nine explicitly editorial thematic connections** between different programme clusters in \`data/curatorial-threads.json\`; these never masquerade as factual person-to-person historical connections.
- Wander: free drag, wheel/pinch zoom and visible scale controls, touch/keyboard affordances, object focus and related-image navigation.
- Index: jump between moments, bilingual archive search, deep entry into source objects.
- Reading route: the chapter text and source images remain accessible without camera movement.
- Source/media build uses exact original extensions; cannot accidentally reinterpret PNGs as JPEGs.
