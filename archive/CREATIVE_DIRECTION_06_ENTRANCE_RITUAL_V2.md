# Creative Direction 06 — Entrance Ritual V2
## Darb Archive / The Archive Unfurls — الأرشيف بيتفرد

**Status:** EXECUTION-READY ART DIRECTION / NOT YET IMPLEMENTED IN THE VERCEL PREVIEW  
**Project continuity:** Develop **inside the existing Darb Archive** on \`research/darb-archive-living-memory-20261109\`. Preserve the current spatial source atlas, 77 cataloged source records, 72 visible archive objects, 16 guided camera moments, nine editorial thematic threads, Archive Compass, object encounters, and reversible navigation. No new standalone website.  
**Creative origin:** Omar's idea of **an Ottoman-inflected carpet unfurling while three eyes create an emergent D**.  
**North star:** A visitor experiences a physical act of discovering memory, rather than watching a graphic designer animate a logo.

---

## 01 / The idea — الفكرة

**The archive isn't displayed. It is unfolded.**

**Not:** "DARB" lettering on top of a rug texture, a carpet stock photo, an eye icon, a grand dark hero and a button.

**Instead:** A real-feeling textile exists first as an object in space. Unfurling applies tension to its warp/weft. Three distinct apertures or *eyes* are exposed by that tension. Only from a particular camera vantage does the negative space and pull of thread briefly read as **D**. The fabric does **not** transform into a different website; it reveals the same archive world that is already there.

The conceptual chain must have a physical reason:

\`woven field → tension → three apertures → emergent D → threads become paths → archival images appear → visitor chooses a trace\`

If any step can be deleted without changing what the next step means, that step is decoration and must be reconsidered.

**What is specifically Darb about this?** Darb's relationship to Fustat's maker/craft communities and contemporary art is documented in contemporary writing; the material language therefore reflects **making and encounter**, not a claimed historic Ottoman rug held by Darb. [Context: Ahram Weekly 2023, Khatt Foundation Darb 1718.] The woven object is a new commissioned creative metaphor—not a historical artifact or provenance claim.

## 02 / The object — السجادة مش خلفية

### Material reference
Look at **Cairo-attributed Ottoman-period carpets** (not merely generic Turkish/Ushak illustrations): The Met collection **17.120.137**, **1971.263.2**, **41.190.257**. These show relations between local Egyptian weaving practice and Ottoman design. References:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/446957
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/452309
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/450466

These sources establish a *historical design vocabulary*, not permission to reproduce museum photography in the production site, and not provenance of Darb objects.

### Textile art-direction properties
- An irregular **object edge** and believable thickness; the cloth is not a full-screen, flat page.
- A pattern that can be pulled apart into **strands**: border rhythm, central repeating motifs, asymmetry in wear and minor repaired seams.
- Surface must disclose **fiber, knot, fold, worn pile and directionality** in changing light, instead of universal canvas noise.
- Avoid picturesque kitsch: no ornate gold UI, lanterns, stock bazaar props, generic Orientalism.
- Deliberately limited starting palette: near-black unlit room, aged madder/rust red, worn indigo/teal, muted flax, charcoal thread. **Not** "everything dark green" from the previous opening. Color is driven by the textile and subsequent source-object media.
- Keep the rest of Darb's archive hidden until the act of unfurling gives it a meaningful entrance. No floating photo cards behind the unrolled rug.

### Asset requirement, before a final shader
For production we need either an **originally commissioned weave design** plus artwork-grade material capture, or a licensed/approved source textile photograph scanned well enough for cloth depth and detail. A procedural mesh can establish a physical prototype, but **must not be passed off as a historic carpet**. A museum reference image is for research unless its rights are separately confirmed.

## 03 / The three eyes — العيون مش 3 أيقونات

The three eyes are not menu categories and not three identical symbols. They are **three different ways a surface becomes able to look back**, deliberately ambiguous enough for the visitor to read.

| Eye | Material event | Possible reading (editorial interpretation, not asserted fact) | Visual rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Witness / الشاهد | An opening appears because a row of stitches releases under tensile load. | Someone saw and experienced what remains in the archive. | Uneven, **stitched** contour; no ready-made iris icon. |
| II — Retained / الحافظ | A second eye becomes visible as a worn layer slides against another; something behind the fabric is seen. | A memory continues in material evidence. | Read via **depth, layering and translucency**. |
| III — Visitor / الزائر | The third is initially a dark negative space that gains shape through the camera's alignment or viewer's action. | The viewer adds a new angle. | Only becomes legible through **perspective/participation**. |

These are **three competing material treatments**, not three mandatory motifs that all animate one after another. Their meanings remain creative hypotheses, not a claim that the historic eye motif carries these assigned meanings. Test with still frames and museum-style reading, not just an animation demo.

**D recognition rule:** The eyes must sit at three meaningful tension points. The form of D is readable because of:
1. One continuous tensioned edge / spine,
2. A curved *boundary of missing weave*,
3. Three eye apertures holding the structure in place.

An actual alphabet-shaped drawn SVG is unacceptable as the final solution. It can be used in internal wire studies only. No automatic "D" caption announces what visitors should discover.

## 04 / Dramaturgy — حركات لها سبب مش ست سكاشن

This is a **single spatial passage**, not six sections in one scroll page.

| Beat | What the visitor perceives | What physically causes next motion | Visitor agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unlit / سكون** | Darkness, one material edge in near-silhouette, almost no information. | A minute pull at the fringe implies a hidden extent. | At any time, tap/scroll/drag to begin; a quiet "Enter archive" bypass remains available. |
| **Unfurl / انفتاح** | A rolled portion starts revealing the woven plane, edge thickness and worn pattern under grazing light. | The cloth unrolls **because its edge moves**; warp and weft strain, not because a mask slides sideways. | Guided movement but responsive to input; pause cannot trap visitor. |
| **Tension / شد** | At different depths, first two eyes emerge as places where the weave shifts, wears or opens. | Cloth deformation creates material apertures. | Looking/pointer movement offers subtle parallax; reduced motion freezes in a legible composition. |
| **Recognition / اكتشاف** | Third eye becomes visible in alignment, and the silhouette *briefly* reads D. | Camera moves to a narrow alignment, not a separate logo overlay. | The D can be noticed or missed; exploration remains possible. |
| **Passage / عبور** | Threads peel away from the textile and continue into spatial archival relationships. The material is still visually connected to the archive for a moment. | An actual strand becomes one of the existing source-backed paths. | Choose a visible trace/source object. |
| **Arrival / وصول** | Archive objects, real images, and the familiar free-wander world gradually resolve. The entrance cloth recedes, not vanishes on a hard cut. | Same camera coordinates and state transition. | Full wander, guided story, and Archive Compass remain accessible; returning does not replay by force. |

**No mandatory loading wait.** The beats are governed by meaningful events and readiness, not a hard-coded 18-second movie. Movement has authored pacing but remains skippable. Silence is a state; don't launch audio without visitor consent.

### One unbroken physical metaphor
The border of a rug cannot merely dissolve into web-like lines with an opacity fade. It should be conceptually clear **which woven threads become which archival paths**. Pick one or two actual source objects and their documented/labelled link to anchor the first transformation.

**Prototype anchor:** \`Tales of Fustat\`, real source objects **#39–42** from the existing PDF p.11; they can appear as images reached along threads, but the textile itself **is not** asserted to be one of these archived objects.

## 05 / Spatial composition — الكاميرا مش بتقدم عنوان

- Opening is a **close, oblique viewpoint of a material artifact**, not a centered symmetrical logo, and not the current D on a full-screen green photo collage.
- Only after the unfolding should the overall D projection become perceptible.
- Camera *first follows the textile*, then witnesses the three eyes, then enters a real aperture and emerges inside the archived image world.
- Negative space in the frame is for **tension and perception**, not for filling with an arbitrary headline.
- Branding should be deferred until the world has taught us what DARB is. If a control is needed, keep it small, contextual and operable.
- To integrate with the existing Archive Compass, the last stage must land inside the same world coordinate system and must preserve the previous guided/story or free-wander route.
- At the end of a visitor's path, the same textile may reappear transformed by the connections they actually followed. This is a **future ending study**, not part of the initial prototype and not a reset to a new splash screen.

## 06 / Sound — الصوت يكشف المادة

**No automatic soundtrack, no ticking UI, no cinematic whoosh for every movement.**

Potential authored sound vocabulary (requires original or duly licensed recordings):
- Cloth tension: close, restrained, fiber-on-fiber friction.
- Eye 1 release: a slight imperfection, almost a break in texture; **not** an eyelid blink sample.
- Eye 2: near-silent change in acoustic distance.
- Third eye / camera alignment: a conscious **absence** of sound.
- Crossing into the archive: the material sound gradually yields to contextual sound **only where actual recordings exist**.

Sound enabled only by explicit visitor action. Persist mute status during the visit and offer a silent exhibition mode. The sound layer can be implemented after the visual passage works; it must not be used to disguise motion that doesn't read.

## 07 / Technical method — prototype without faking the artifact

**Keep production architecture intact.** The existing \`web/app.mjs\`, \`src/world-layout.mjs\`, source catalog, and Stage 05 Compass remain the authoritative world.

### Interaction module contract
Introduce a tightly scoped, side-effect-light \`entrance-ritual-v2\` state machine with:
- \`phase\`: \`unlit | unfurl | tension | recognition | passage | arrived\`
- \`sourceThread\`: the documented object/pair used to bridge into the world
- \`progress\`: normalized, visually readable state for the current phase
- \`quiet/reducedMotion\`: changes presentation, **not** the available content or controls
- \`enterArchive()\`: irreversibly completes the introduction for this visit without destroying underlying world history
- \`revisitEntrance()\`: optional deliberate replay; never forced after completing an archive route

### Physical rendering direction
- A genuine mesh or carefully constructed plane system with visible **roll/edge geometry**; a procedural weave shader can support the **interaction proof**, not final source photography.
- Separate *cloth unroll deformation* from *camera position* so the difference between movement of object and movement of camera remains perceptible.
- Three eye treatments should have different material causes, not share an identical opacity/scale keyframe preset.
- Use **authored geometry / actual references** for the slit-to-D silhouette, not an SVG letter layer.
- Pass an explicit **source-path ID** from the textile to the existing archive world and restore focus/camera state on exit.
- Prefer progressive image decode and source-specific image resolution; avoid decoding 77 large assets immediately just to show a 1-second title.
- \`prefers-reduced-motion\`: keep woven plane, three apertures and path clearly legible as a still composition with a direct enter action.
- Keyboard: Enter/Space to continue; Escape/skip is consistent. Touch, trackpad, scroll and kiosk pointer each need a usable path.

### Integration boundary
Use an **opt-in experiment flag within the current application** during the art-direction acceptance stage. Do not replace the current Vercel Preview's opening until the V2 passage is visually reviewed. This is not a second website. Don't ship an unreviewed vector rug as if it were the commissioned textile.

## 08 / Production sequence — what we actually build first

**Slice 06A — cloth/unfurl and one eye only**
1. Material board: three museum references + original Darb workshop/craft photos + first 2D pattern study. Record what is being borrowed and what is newly authored.
2. Small moving textile prototype, **without header, hero, navigation panels or words** in the stage. Prove the fabric has convincing edge tension and depth.
3. First eye must result from the material action. A frozen still should look intentionally composed.
4. Capture at 1440×900 and 390×844 with readable edge/material detail. Compare stills to actual reference studies.

**Slice 06B — three eyes and the emergent D**
5. Two additional eyes each from a different textile behavior.
6. Make the D recognizable *only from one useful view* with no drawn letter. Check that the three eyes hold the geometry.
7. Reduce motion and still-mode test: the whole conceptual chain must remain legible.

**Slice 06C — entrance into existing world**
8. Tie one released thread to a real Tales of Fustat source ID.
9. Transition the same camera into the existing spatial atlas, **not through a blank/loading cut**.
10. Return/replay, AR/EN, touch, keyboard, manual skip, state preservation and performance.

**Slice 06D — ending resonance (later)**
11. Let the strands of a real visitor path reshape the textile or eye geometry.
12. Only after the opening and artwork encounters are fully polished, make the final moment reframe the first moment.

## 09 / Creative quality gates

- [ ] Does the opening still make sense with the word "DARB" completely hidden?
- [ ] Can a viewer tell the object has thickness and woven structure, not just an animated image texture?
- [ ] Are the three eyes **visually distinct**, tied to different acts of material change, and not clip-art?
- [ ] Is D genuinely discovered through camera and geometry rather than drawn as a logo?
- [ ] Can the visitor choose an early archive entrance without waiting for the entire choreography?
- [ ] Does a real thread lead to a real object without a hard cut or old-style card?
- [ ] Is there an equally intentional quiet/reduced-motion alternative?
- [ ] Does the scene work in Arabic, English, desktop, mobile and eventually exhibition framing?
- [ ] Are material references and permissions documented separately from archived Darb works?
- [ ] Is this recognizably **Darb**, rather than Getty's sphere, Oculi's map book, or an ornamental "Eastern" title animation?
- [ ] Has the actual browser interaction been **filmed and critiqued**, not only unit-tested?
- [ ] Have we waited to scale until one individual entrance beat actually meets the quality bar?

**Release rule:** An impressive still is not enough. An animated intro alone is not enough. The passage must establish the same physical language all the way to a first artifact encounter, then transition back to the existing free-wander world with no loss of agency.
