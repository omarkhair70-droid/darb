# International cultural website case-study atlas
Research verified against award bodies, institutions and the studios involved, as of **2026-11-02**. Awards are **not** inferred from aesthetics.

## 01 — National Gallery Imaginarium (UK, 2025)
**Recognition:** The Webby Awards 2026 **Winner** in Cultural Institutions; 2025 Lovie Silver; 2026 European Design Silver (institution reports).
**Links:** https://winners.webbyawards.com/2026/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/cultural-institutions/380455/national-gallery-imaginarium | https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/visiting/virtual-gallery/national-gallery-imaginarium | https://www.q42.nl/en/work/imaginarium-national-gallery
- **Problem:** let a remote audience encounter paintings deeply rather than browse facsimiles.
- **Material:** paintings, curatorial questions, commissioned sound/poetry.
- **Creative decision:** a virtual “slow looking” dialogue, light 3D space, chosen artworks.
- **Technical means (maker-disclosed):** Three.js, React, React Three Fiber, Micrio, Sanity, Firebase, AI-enhanced animation.
- **Transfer to Darb:** artwork/photograph → guided observation → human story, with optional ambience.
- **Do not copy:** floating paintings, generative alteration, VR-like entry, paid AI features without purpose.
- **Proposed Darb experiment:** 1 photo from a known exhibition, 3 evidence-based guided questions, credits, an opt-in local sound field, static fallback.
- **Decision risk:** intimate interaction scale vs Darb's community/archive scale.

## 02 — Getty + Resn: Tracing Art (USA/NZ, 2025)
**Recognition:** Awwwards Site of the Day Jul 22, 2025 (award archive); Webby 2026 Cultural Institutions nominee.
**Links:** https://www.getty.edu/tracingart | https://winners.webbyawards.com/2026/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/cultural-institutions/360410/tracing-art | https://www.commarts.com/project/38649/tracing-art
- **Problem:** make dense, incomplete provenance records understandable and emotionally relevant.
- **Material:** linked people, locations, owners, decades, objects and documentation.
- **Decision:** immersive editorial chapters woven through factual data; fluid transitions but source/detail remain.
- **Technical/production evidence:** makers reported **400 images**, **16 collaborators**, **13 weeks** from concept to launch; not a trivial microsite.
- **Transfer:** Darb's artistic history can be navigated through linked *person / work / event / place*.
- **Do not copy:** archival visual tropes, data animations with no evidentiary meaning.
- **Experiment:** truthful Something Else 2023 timeline connecting a site, curators and art images with one related project.

## 03 — Nordiska Museet (Sweden, 2025)
**Recognition:** **Webby Winner Cultural Institutions 2025**, Awwwards Honorable Mention Jul 17 2025, 2025 Lovie Gold and MUSE Platinum (institution).
**Links:** https://www.nordiskamuseet.se/ | https://winners.webbyawards.com/2025/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/cultural-institutions/324196/nordiska-museet | https://www.nordiskamuseet.se/om-museet/utmarkelser-och-priser/
- **Problem:** an inviting, modern cultural gateway that still communicates practical visit details.
- **Material:** people and historical objects, exhibitions, collection image and dates.
- **Decision:** distinct typography, shape-aware framing, strong high-contrast colour, useful modular page types.
- **Transfer:** an unusually confident institution-facing design can still be usable.
- **Do not copy:** burgundy/red, motifs or exact masked hero typography.
- **Experiment:** high-contrast navigational hero pairing authentic Darb image with today's event, fully functional on mobile and Arabic.

## 04 — Rijksmuseum (Netherlands, 2020)
**Recognition:** European Design Gold 2021, additional awards disclosed by Q42.
**Links:** https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en | https://awards.europeandesign.org/winner/248792 | https://www.q42.nl/en/work/rijks-website1
- **Problem:** make deep collections and curatorial research enjoyable to enter without physical visit.
- **Decision:** ultra-large artwork, an editorial Stories platform, deep zoom, audience-specific journeys.
- **Transfer:** one artwork at a time, image/detail-first with context, curated story series.
- **Do not copy:** exact visual template, arbitrary hero-scale works without credit or utility.
- **Experiment:** Darb artist page and one exhibition's images with zoom and attributed story fragments.

## 05 — London Museum (UK, 2025)
**Recognition:** Museums + Heritage Awards **Best Use of Digital – UK winner 2025**, Webby AI nominee.
**Links:** https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/ | https://awards.museumsandheritage.com/awards/2025-winners/best-use-of-digital-uk-5/ | https://winners.webbyawards.com/2025/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-use-of-ai/322792/london-museums-new-website-is-using-ai-to-power-content-relationships-between-objects-and-stories
- **Problem:** expose relationships between stories and objects at scale.
- **Decision:** structured content linking; award recognizes creative applied AI.
- **Transfer:** relations and metadata *before* AI. Human-curated tagging and relevance first.
- **Do not copy:** introduce AI recommendation for a 77-image starter corpus without a validated use case.

## 06 — Museum London (Canada, 2026)
**Recognition:** 2026 RGD Element Award, User Experience; verified award case.
**Link:** https://rgd.ca/hiring-designers/award-winners/2026-branding-award-winners/element-awards/museum-london-website
- **Problem:** buried programmes and aging publishing workflow.
- **Decision:** What’s On now/next/archives; rich exhibition pages with image, audio and context; events filter and clear registration.
- **Transfer:** editorial visual innovation must coexist with useful CMS-driven programme discovery.
- **Do not copy:** the museum's own navigation structure verbatim.
- **Experiment:** Darb today/upcoming/cancelled, archivally connected programme list.

## 07 — Oculi Mundi (UK/Netherlands, 2023)
**Recognition:** European Design Award 2024 per Q42; **2026 Webby Honoree** in Cultural Institutions.
**Links:** https://oculi-mundi.com | https://www.q42.nl/en/work/oculimundi | https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/cultural-institutions
- **Problem:** provide a digital global experience of rare globes, maps and books.
- **Decision:** spatial metaphor derived **directly** from globe/map objects; immersive archive.
- **Technical maker disclosure:** Next.js, Tailwind, TypeScript, Sanity; case also includes immersive 3D work.
- **Transfer:** only use dimensional interaction if the cultural artifact itself is inherently spatial; maybe a *real* workshop map/architecture with verified data.
- **Do not copy:** rotating cosmos because “international” sounds futuristic.

## Judging logic (not certification)
Awwwards judges design, usability, creativity, content; Webby categories vary. Use **recognition as evidence of good execution**, not a recipe or guarantee for future awards.

## Detailed scoring worksheet
For each case collect actual screenshot/documented feature; score (1–5) for:
1. Purpose-led originality
2. Source/artwork respect
3. Narrative comprehension
4. Practical utility
5. Mobile & AR/EN portability
6. Interaction restraint
7. Performance accessibility
8. Editorial maintainability

Run browser audit before claiming individual case's technical performance. Sources here show described design, not independently benchmarked Core Web Vitals.
